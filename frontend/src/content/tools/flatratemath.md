description: >-
  flatratemath prices three ways to buy software the same way: a one-time fixed
  bid, a fully loaded salaried hire, and a flat monthly subscription. Give it
  your expected scope, months, and request count and it reduces every lane to
  total cost per delivered request, salary included at its full loaded cost. One
  Python file, standard library only, three modes: single bid,
  bid-vs-hire-vs-flat flags, or a CSV compare that ranks the whole decision. The
  flat-fee side of that math lives at poketdev.com.
